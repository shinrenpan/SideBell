Two weeks in the review queue. SideBell is live.

It's a call bell for people who can't speak or move easily. They tap a button on the iPad beside their bed — their caregiver's phone raises an alarm and says out loud what's needed.

Bluetooth only. No server, no account, no subscription. It keeps working when the Wi-Fi doesn't, which in a home care setting is most of the time.

Every control is a single tap. No swiping, no long press — so iOS Eye Tracking and Dwell Control can drive it directly, without the app implementing any of its own. VoiceOver is fully supported.

Built for RevenueCat Shipaton 2026. Every feature is free — the tip options unlock nothing.
